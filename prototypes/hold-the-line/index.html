Lake Regillus · 496 BC

Hold the Line

The Latin League marches on Rome. Stop every warrior before he reaches your camp.

  • Drag a unit onto a square, or tap it and then tap a square.
  • Supply carts bring rations. Tap the grain to collect it, and grain also falls from the sky.
  • Slingers shoot down their whole lane. Triarii kneel behind their shields and hold the line.
  • If a Latin reaches the camp, the equites charge down that lane, but only once.
