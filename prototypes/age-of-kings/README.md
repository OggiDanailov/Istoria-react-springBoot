# Ave Caesar game prototype

A single-page prototype for every chapter generated by `tools/content-gen`: the chapter text, its test made of
mini-games, and its battle. Navigation: Ancient Rome → era (Kingdom, Early Republic, …) → chapter.
**How the test, the points and the battles work: see [GAME_DESIGN.md](../../GAME_DESIGN.md).**

| Game | Teaches |
|---|---|
| Chronology Jam | order of events (tap or drag onto the timeline road) |
| Match Pairs | people, terms and what they did or mean (including a Latin Words round) |
| True or False | quick facts (swipe or buttons) |
| Odd One Out | connections between people and events |
| Who Am I? | people, from progressive clues |
| Map Drop | where places were (real coordinates, central Italy) |
| Legion Quiz | multiple-choice questions, drawn from the chapter's whole pool for each attempt of the test |
| Legion Run | practice: steer through the gate with the right answer (not part of the test) |

Also in the prototype:
- **The chapter test**: every round of every game; first answers count, weighted by how hard they are to guess.
  The bar at the top shows progress and the score; a finished test opens the chapter's battle.
- **The battle**: Hold the Line (`../hold-the-line`, embedded by the build) with the army the score earned.
- **The chronicle**: the full chapter text in a slide-up panel, with Latin terms in red, maps (`maps.json`), and
  links from every wrong answer to its section. **Key facts** (`keyfacts.json`): people, dates and Latin words.
- **Feedback** after every round: owners and members of the owner's organization save it to the page;
  everyone else copies a short summary to paste into a message.
- **Review mode** (progress sheet: tap the score in the top bar): every chapter open, each chapter's questions with
  answers, and every battle playable at a chosen score, without changing progress.

Published (private) at https://claude.ai/artifact/7vX45xzWiZhyPC2siymC4j.
To try it locally, open `index.html` in a browser.

## Building

```bash
node build.mjs
```

`build.mjs` reads the chapter order, eras and names from `tools/content-gen/chapters.json`, and each chapter's
`games.json`, `chapter.md` and quiz batches from `content/generated/`, leaves out every item the fact-check flagged,
and writes `index.html` from `template.html` (embedding `../hold-the-line/game.js` and `game.css`). Edit `template.html`,
never `index.html`. Optional per chapter: `keyfacts.json` and `maps.json` next to `chapter.md`.

`sweep.test.mjs` visits every screen of every chapter and reports blank screens or script errors; run it before publishing.
`land.json` holds coastlines of Italy and its neighbours from Natural Earth (public domain) for the map.
